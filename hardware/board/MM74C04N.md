# MM74C04N

The MM74C04N is a hex inverter used for logic inversion, signal conditioning, and interface stabilization around the control and serial section of the board.

## Pinout

```text
             +---U---+
      IN 1 --| 1  14 |-- VCC (+3V to +15V)
     OUT 1 --| 2  13 |-- IN 6
      IN 2 --| 3  12 |-- OUT 6
     OUT 2 --| 4  11 |-- IN 5
      IN 3 --| 5  10 |-- OUT 5
     OUT 3 --| 6   9 |-- IN 4
       GND --| 7   8 |-- OUT 4
             +-------+
```

## Board connections

- The two MM74C04N packages are used as inversion and conditioning stages for interface and control lines.
- Multiple DB25 and 8031 lines are routed through pull-up resistors and these inverter stages before reaching the logic network.

## MM74C04N #1

```ascii
                                     +---U---+
              pin 5 DB25 -> 100 kΩ --| 1  14 |-- VCC (+3V to +15V)
            (P1.6) P1.6 pin 7 8031 --| 2  13 |-+ 100 kΩ - GND
                                     |       | +-10 kΩ - pin 2 DB9 
             pin 17 DB25 -> 100 kΩ --| 3  12 |-- pin 10 8031 (RXD) P3.0
                        pin 8 8031 --| 4  11 |-- pin 6
Emergency OFF pin 4 DB25 -> 100 kΩ --| 5  10 |-- pin 12 8031 (INT0) P3.2
                            pin 11 --| 6   9 |-+ 100 kΩ - GND
                                     |       | + 10 kΩ - pin 4 DB9
                               GND --| 7   8 |-- pin 14 8031 (T0) P3.4
                                     +-------+
```

## MM74C04N #2

```ascii
                                    +---U---+
             pin 8 DB25 -> 100 kΩ --| 1  14 |-- VCC (+3V to +15V)
                       pin 1 8031 --| 2  13 |-- 100 kΩ <- pin 20 DB25 
             pin 7 DB25 -> 100 kΩ --| 3  12 |-- pin 2 8031
                       pin 3 8031 --| 4  11 |-- 100 kΩ <- pin 19 DB25
             pin 6 DB25 -> 100 kΩ --| 5  10 |-- pin 4 8031
                       pin 5 8031 --| 6   9 |-- 100 kΩ <- pin 18 DB25
                              GND --| 7   8 |-- pin 6 8031
                                    +-------+
```
