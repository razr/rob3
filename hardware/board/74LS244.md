# 74LS244

The 74LS244 is an octal line driver used to buffer the board's signal lines and isolate the 8255/8031 logic from the external DB25 interface.

## Pinout

```text
                       TOP VIEW
                      +----U----+
  1G* (output enable) | 1    20 | VCC (+5V)
      1A1 input       | 2    19 | 2G* (output enable)
      2Y4 output      | 3    18 | 1Y1 output
      1A2 input       | 4    17 | 2A4 input
      2Y3 output      | 5    16 | 1Y2 output
      1A3 input       | 6    15 | 2A3 input
      2Y2 output      | 7    14 | 1Y3 output
      1A4 input       | 8    13 | 2A2 input
      2Y1 output      | 9    12 | 1Y4 output
       GND            |10    11 | 2A1 input
                      +---------+
```

## Board connections

```text
                          TOP VIEW
                          +---U---+
                    GND ──| 1   20|── VCC (+5V)
            8255 pin 19 ──| 2   19|── GND
            DB25 pin 22 ──| 3   18|── DB25 pin 12
            8255 pin 18 ──| 4   17|── 8255 pin 24
            DB25 pin 11 ──| 5   16|── DB25 pin 25
            8255 pin 20 ──| 6   15|── 8255 pin 21
            DB25 pin 10 ──| 7   14|── DB25 pin 24
            8255 pin 22 ──| 8   13|── 8255 pin 23
             DB25 pin 9 ──| 9   12|── DB25 pin 23
                    GND ──|10   11|── 8255 pin 25
                          +-------+
```

## Notes

- The driver provides a clean external bus interface for DB25 signals.
- It is used when the controller needs to drive external I/O without loading the internal logic rails.
