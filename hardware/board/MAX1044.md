# MAX1044

The MAX1044 is a charge-pump voltage inverter used to generate the negative supply rail required around the serial and analog interface conditioning circuitry.

## Pinout

```text
         +---U---+
   BOOST | 1   8 | V+ (input voltage)
    CAP+ | 2   7 | OSC
     GND | 3   6 | LV
    CAP- | 4   5 | VOUT (inverted output)
         +-------+
```

## Board connections

```text
               MAX1044
             +---U---+
             | 1   8 | <--- +9V input
   +-------  | 2   7 |
   |   GND   | 3   6 |
   +-------  | 4   5 | ---> -9V output
[ 10µF cap ] +-------+
```

## Notes

- It creates the negative rail needed for the RS-232/analog interface stage.
- The generated negative voltage is used in conjunction with the M34004 amplifier chain.
