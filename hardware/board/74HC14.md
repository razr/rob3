# 74HC14

The 74HC14 is a high-speed CMOS Schmitt-trigger inverter used to clean noisy or slow digital edges before they reach the rest of the board logic.

## Pinout

```text
                    TOP VIEW
                  +----U----+
    1A (input 1)  | 1    14 | VCC (+5V)
    1Y (output 1) | 2    13 | 6A (input 6)
    2A (input 2)  | 3    12 | 6Y (output 6)
    2Y (output 2) | 4    11 | 5A (input 5)
    3A (input 3)  | 5    10 | 5Y (output 5)
    3Y (output 3) | 6     9 | 4A (input 4)
      GND         | 7     8 | 4Y (output 4)
                  +---------+
```

## Board connections

```text
   8031 pin 28, pins 1, 2 tied together | 2┘   13 | 8031 pin 29
                            SRAM pin 20 | 3    12 | 8031 pin 17, 8255 pin 5
 74LS138 pin 5, pins 4, 5 tied together | 4┐  ┌11 | pins 9, 10, 11 tied together
 74LS138 pin 5, pins 4, 5 tied together | 5┘  ├10 | pins 9, 10, 11 tied together
                                     NC | 6   └─9 | pins 9, 10, 11 tied together
                                    GND | 7     8 | EPROM pin 22
                                        +---------+
```

## Notes

- This IC acts as a logic-conditioning stage for control and address timing.
- Its Schmitt-trigger inputs make it useful for debouncing and waveform shaping.
